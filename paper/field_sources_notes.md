# Field sources (gathered 14 November 2026)

1. Fervo Energy (Sparrow, M.), 13 Apr 2026. "Enhanced Geothermal Has Been Proven at Scale. Here's What Two Years of Production Data Show."
   https://fervoenergy.com/enhanced-geothermal-has-been-proven-at-scale-heres-what-two-years-of-production-data-show/
   Project Red: commercial operation from 30 Oct 2023; >614 days on production; uptime 98.4 % excl. surface/grid outages; zero workovers.
   Average circulation ~36 kg/s; peak test 60 kg/s, 3.5 MWe peak gross. Average production temperature 347 F (175 C).
   Flat >500 days, then ~2.5 F (1.4 K) decline; model-predicted onset ~500 days (Gringarten 1975 basis).
   Average gross 2.1 MW, net ~1.4 MW. SRV ~ 975 m (lateral) x 110 m (spacing) x 143 m (fracture height).
   Thermal recovery factor ~20 % to date. Recapture ~70 %. EGS 1.0 ~2 MW/well -> EGS 3.0 ~16 MW gross per production well.
   ThinkGeoEnergy 14 Apr 2026 summary adds: injection temperature ~75 C.
   https://www.thinkgeoenergy.com/fervo-highlights-stable-operations-of-project-red-geothermal-project-after-600-days/
2. Norbeck, J.H., Latimer, T., 2023. Commercial-Scale Demonstration of a First-of-a-Kind Enhanced Geothermal System. EarthArXiv, doi 10.31223/X52X0B.
   37-day crossflow test, >60 L/s (63 L/s), 3.5 MWe; laterals ~3,250 ft; well separation 365 ft; depth ~7,600-7,700 ft; ~191 C;
   75-100 flowing fractures estimated; spinner log fairly uniform; producer not pumped; plant outlet pressure 1500 psi; pump eff 0.80.
   McClure (ResFrac blog 19 Jul 2023): cluster spacing 25 ft; ~100 % perforation efficiency; 300-400 md-ft per fracture.
   https://www.resfrac.com/blog/fervo-energys-project-red-results-are-a-historic-breakthrough-for-geothermal-energy-what-comes-next
3. Cape Station 2024 test. Fervo press release 10 Sep 2024: 30-day test, max 107 kg/s, >10 MW; reservoir >220 C.
   https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240910997008/en
   McClure (ResFrac blog 12 Sep 2024): two injectors bounding one producer; ~450 ft spacing; 5,000 ft laterals; producer >95 kg/s;
   production temperature 383 F (195 C) still rising; injection WHP 2,000-2,300 psi; >9.5 MWe gross less a few MWe parasitic;
   "10-15 years to know with certainty"; 0.015-0.03 kg/s per ft of lateral across Cape, Red, FORGE.
   https://www.resfrac.com/blog/digesting-the-bonkers-incredible-off-the-charts-spectacular-results-from-the-fervo-and-forge-enhanced-geothermal-projects
   McClure, M.W., 2025. Preliminary Analysis of Results from the Utah FORGE Project. Proc. 50th Stanford Geothermal Workshop, SGP-TR-229:
   Cape 93-121 kg/s (Norbeck et al. 2024); FORGE Aug 2024 30-day test 26-28 kg/s over ~1,000 ft stimulated lateral, 429 F at 8,561 ft;
   one stage took 25.8 % of flow; tracer swept volume 4,680 bbl.  https://pangea.stanford.edu/ERE/db/GeoConf/papers/SGW/2025/Mcclure.pdf
4. Cape Station 2026. Fervo 24 Sep 2026: first power; Phase I ~100 MW = three 33 MW GeoBlocks; +400 MW by 2028.
   https://fervoenergy.com/fervo-energy-achieves-first-power-at-cape-station-a-landmark-moment-for-the-future-of-enhanced-geothermal-systems/
   Fervo Q3 2026 results (9 Nov 2026): GeoBlock 1 commercial operation 30 Sep 2026; capacity in operation 36 MW.
   https://ir.fervoenergy.com/news-releases/news-release-details/fervo-energy-reports-third-quarter-2026-results
   Fervo 3.0 design (Q2 2026 call, as cited in GEOPHIRES Cape-8 case study): 7,500 ft laterals, 8-5/8 in casing, ~221 C design point; Phase I 5,000 ft laterals, 7 in casing.
   Gradient: 200 C at 8,500 ft (Fercho et al. 2024); 228.9 C at 9,824 ft (Norbeck et al. 2024) (as cited in GEOPHIRES case study).
5. Singh, A., Galban, G., McClure, M., Briggs, K., Norbeck, J., 2025. Designing the Record-Breaking Enhanced Geothermal System at Project Cape. URTeC 4245311.
   30-year circulation simulations; tighter well spacing -> higher short-term flow, earlier thermal breakthrough; first producer >100 kg/s.
   (GEOPHIRES case study cites it for 500 ft intra-bench well spacing, 30 ft cluster spacing, 3 producers : 2 injectors bench, 300 psi WHP, 120-130 kg/s initial.)
   https://www.resfrac.com/papers/designing-the-record-breaking-enhanced-geothermal-system-at-project-cape
6. Ou, Y., Mondal, S., Savitski, A.A., Sharma, M.M., 2026. Optimizing Cluster Spacing in Enhanced Geothermal Systems. Proc. 51st Stanford Geothermal Workshop, SGP-TR-230.
   Optimum cluster spacing increases with injection rate; high recovery needs uniform fracture permeability; Cape-like triplet model.
   https://pangea.stanford.edu/ERE/db/GeoConf/papers/SGW/2026/Ou.pdf
7. Yearsley, E., 27 Jul 2026. The reservoir will always have its final say. GeoExpro. Critical view: Project Red sustained 1.4 MW net vs 3.5 MW peak;
   thermal decline began; make-up water; Cape first test ~6 MW net after 24 h from three wells. https://geoexpro.com/the-reservoir-will-always-have-its-final-say/
8. GEOPHIRES-X v3.20.1 (git 451c712, 9 Nov 2026): case studies Fervo_Project_Red-2026 (65 fractures de-rated from 75-100, 300 ft height, 365 ft width, 10 m separation, 36 kg/s)
   and Fervo_Project_Cape-8 (107 kg/s per producer, 221 C, 100 m x 305 m fractures 9.8 m apart, 2 redrillings in 30 yr, pumping 17 % of gross).
9. McClure, M. et al., 2023. Thermoelastic fracturing and buoyancy-driven convection: surprising sources of longevity for EGS circulation (preprint).
10. Toews, M. (Eavor), 21 May 2026. Technical Update from Geretsried: What we built, what we learned, and what comes next.
    https://eavor.com/blog/technical-update-from-geretsried-what-we-built-what-we-learned-and-what-comes-next/
    Loop 1: production "currently around 8.5 MWth which is exactly as expected"; six lateral pairs drilled and intersected instead of the
    twelve designed, "four lateral pairs are operating as expected today" (two of the first pairs not cleaned out); "over 2 MWth per lateral
    pair" now, 1.3 MWth after five years; "Over the average thermal duty since startup – 11.8 MWth – the conversion efficiency is roughly
    4.3%" (higher in winter, lower in summer); original design four loops of 12 lateral pairs of about 6 km (about 72 km of reservoir
    exposure) for nameplate 8.2 MW electricity (64 MW thermal), i.e. 1.3 MWth per pair; 2 MWe at Geretsried against 20 MWe for a loop at
    15 km in 30 C/km. Not stated: flow rate, rock temperature, inlet and outlet temperatures, start-up date.
